Thought Studio

A non-clinical space to explore difficult thoughts with honesty and balance. Every step is optional—you can move freely between steps, save partway through, or stop whenever you want.

Step 1 of 5 · Optional

What have you been telling yourself?

Write the thought or belief honestly as it shows up in your mind. You do not need to censor it or immediately prove it wrong.

Step 2 of 5 · All questions optional

Understand the context around the thought

Difficult thoughts usually appear for a reason. Explore any of the questions below that feel helpful right now.

Step 3 of 5 · Optional

What supports this thought, and what might it be leaving out?

A thought does not have to be 100% false to be incomplete. Notice where facts end and where harsh generalizations begin.

Lens: Observable facts vs. assumptions Lens: One hard moment vs. your whole character
Step 4 of 5 · Optional

What is a more balanced way to understand this?

Aim for something believable and grounded—not forced positivity.

Step 5 of 5 · Optional

What is one kind or practical thing you could do next?

You can leave this blank if simply reflecting is enough for today.
